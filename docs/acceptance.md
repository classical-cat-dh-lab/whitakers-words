# Release verification

The current [1.0.3 application release](release-1.0.3.md) uses the unchanged
[stable 1.0 engine](release-1.0.md). [Compatibility and tested scope](compatibility.md)
is the single current account of engine qualification, limits and reproduction.

The [release manifest](legacy-release.json) fixes the 28 engine/data/adapter paths
and their hashes. The [pre-release checkpoint](legacy-checkpoint.md) and
[0.3 backend baseline](backend-baseline.md) retain historical identities.

Earlier acceptance reports describe their own candidates. The 109,001 differences
reported for 0.2 were subsequently resolved; they are not failures of the current
release. Exact historical reports remain in the
[immutable version 1.0.0 source](https://github.com/classical-cat-dh-lab/whitakers-words/blob/v1.0.0/docs/acceptance.md).
